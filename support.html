Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create my first invoice?

Set up your business profile first (logo, business details), then add a client, and you're ready to create an invoice from the Invoices tab.

Can I manage more than one business?

Yes. InvoicePro supports multiple business profiles under a single account — switch between them from the business selector.

How do I create a quotation?

Quotations work the same way as invoices — add a client, select or add items, and generate a professional quotation you can share or convert to an invoice later.

How do I share an invoice with a client?

Open the invoice and use the Share option to export it as a PDF and send it via WhatsApp, email, or any other app on your device.

Does InvoicePro support AI invoice scanning?

Yes. You can scan or upload a document and InvoicePro will automatically extract and fill in the invoice details for you.

How do I delete my account or data?

See our Privacy Policy for details on data handling and account deletion.
