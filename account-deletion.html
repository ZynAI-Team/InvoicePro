Delete Your InvoicePro Account

InvoicePro allows you to permanently delete your account and all associated data directly from within the app.

How to Delete Your Account

Delete from the App

  1. Open the InvoicePro app and sign in.
  2. Navigate to Settings → Account.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the deletion when prompted.
⚠️ Warning

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Data Deleted

When your account is deleted, the following information is permanently removed:

  • User account profile (email, username, login credentials)
  • Businesses
  • Clients
  • Invoices
  • Quotations
  • Predefined items
  • Uploaded business logos
  • Uploaded files
  • AI-assisted invoice parsing history

Data Retention Period

  • Personal data: Deleted immediately after account deletion.
  • No additional retention period applies beyond the minimal anonymous records described above.

Third-Party Services

InvoicePro uses Amazon S3 to store uploaded files.

When you delete your account:

  • All uploaded files are deleted from Amazon S3.
  • If deletion temporarily fails because of a service issue, the system automatically retries until deletion succeeds.
