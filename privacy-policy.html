Privacy Policy for InvoicePro: Invoices Handler

Last Updated: July 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how InvoicePro ("we", "our", or "the app") collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use our mobile application and related services.

By using InvoicePro, you agree to the collection and use of your information as described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account Information

When you register or sign in, we may collect:

  • Email address
  • Username (if provided)
  • Authentication identifier provided by Amazon Cognito
  • Password (handled securely by AWS Cognito. We do not store your password.)

2.2 Business Information

To provide invoicing features, we collect the information you choose to enter, including:

  • Business name
  • Business address
  • Business phone number
  • Business email address
  • Business logo (uploaded image)
  • Bank/account details (such as ACN, BSB, and account number)
  • Tax settings
  • Currency settings

2.3 Client Information

You may store information about your own customers, including:

  • Client name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Address

2.4 Invoice and Quotation Data

We collect information related to invoices and quotations, including:

  • Invoice and quotation line items
  • Amounts
  • Dates
  • Statuses
  • Payment records
  • Notes and descriptions

2.5 AI-Assisted Features

If you use our AI-powered features, we process:

  • Text submitted for invoice parsing
  • Uploaded documents used for invoice extraction
  • AI-generated invoice details

These features use Google Gemini API.

Your submitted invoice content is not used to train AI models.

2.6 Uploaded Files

Business logos and uploaded documents are securely stored using Amazon Web Services (AWS S3).

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Create, edit, and manage invoices and quotations
  • Generate invoice PDFs
  • Send invoices and notifications via email
  • Provide AI-powered invoice parsing and note generation
  • Display dashboards and business reports
  • Improve application performance and security
  • Respond to customer support requests

We do not sell your personal information.

We do not share your business, client, or invoice data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

4. Information Sharing

We only share information with trusted service providers necessary to operate InvoicePro.

Service Provider Purpose Data Shared
Amazon Web Services (Cognito) User Authentication Email, authentication identifiers
Amazon Web Services (S3) Secure File Storage Business logos and uploaded documents
Google Gemini API AI Invoice Parsing Text and documents submitted for parsing
Email/SMTP Provider Sending invoices and notifications Recipient email address and invoice content

We may also disclose information when required by law or when necessary to protect the rights, safety, and security of InvoicePro, our users, or the public.

5. Public Invoice Sharing

If you generate and share a public invoice link, anyone with that link can access the invoice without signing in.

You are responsible for sharing public invoice links only with intended recipients.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information.

These include:

  • HTTPS/TLS encryption for all data transmitted between the app and our servers
  • Secure password management through Amazon Cognito
  • Restricted access to production systems
  • Secure cloud storage using AWS

Although we implement industry-standard security measures, no method of electronic storage or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention

We retain your information while your account remains active.

If you request account deletion:

  • Your account information is permanently deleted.
  • Business information is removed.
  • Client information is removed.
  • Invoices and quotations are deleted.
  • Payment records are deleted.
  • Notifications are deleted.
  • AI request history is deleted.
  • Uploaded files (including business logos) are removed from cloud storage.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access your personal information
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Withdraw consent where applicable

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the details below.

9. Children's Privacy

InvoicePro is intended for adults and business users aged 18 years and older. It is not designed or marketed for children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that personal information has been collected from a child in violation of applicable law, we will take reasonable steps to delete that information promptly.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

Any changes will be published on this page with an updated Last Updated date.

Continued use of InvoicePro after changes become effective constitutes acceptance of the revised Privacy Policy.
